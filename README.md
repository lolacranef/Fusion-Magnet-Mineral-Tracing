# Fusion-Magnet-Mineral-Tracing

A stress test of the rules that should stop human rights abuses behind fusion superconducting magnets: how each abuse gets through, and who keeps the gap open.

**Live site:** https://lolacranef.github.io/Fusion-Magnet-Mineral-Tracing/

Coilchain tests 13 rules (disclosure and due diligence laws, industry schemes, border bans, sanctions and state controls) against the documented abuses in 4 mining regions that supply the yttrium, tin and niobium in fusion magnets: Kachin and Shan & Wa in Myanmar, Bangka Belitung in Indonesia, and Rubaya in DR Congo. A rule is tested only where the abuse's real route passes it. Of 45 tests, none hold: in 26 the abuse gets through a specific loophole, and governments open or keep open 18 of the failures.
