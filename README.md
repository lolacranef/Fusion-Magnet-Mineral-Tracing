# Coilchain: Fusion Magnet Mineral Tracing

Which laws reach the mines behind fusion superconducting magnets, and what each gap lets through.

**Live site:** https://lolacranef.github.io/Fusion-Magnet-Mineral-Tracing/

Coilchain checks 13 rules (US, EU, German, Japanese, international and Chinese) against 4 high-risk mining regions that supply the yttrium, tin and niobium in fusion magnets: Kachin and Shan & Wa in Myanmar, Bangka Belitung in Indonesia, and Rubaya in DR Congo. Only 4 of the 52 checks find a rule that fully applies today.
